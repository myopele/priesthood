From There to 52 Weeks

This Global Priesthood image becomes a full year of spiritual formation, ritual discipline, ancestral alignment, divination practice, and priestly embodiment. This combines Ifa and Khemetic belief systems as practiced by some in the African Diaspora of North America.

The year is organized around the sacred seasonal gates on the North American Continent: Spring Equinox, Summer Solstice, Fall Equinox, and Winter Solstice. These gates index the major initiation pathways: Ilekes, Warriors, Hand of Ifá, Priesthood Crowning with Khemtic Implementation.

The Four Seasonal Gates

The course year is not only academic. It is ritualized through the solar year. Each seasonal gate marks a major shift in consciousness, responsibility, and priestly readiness and reversibility.

Spring Equinox

Gate I: Devotion & Ilekes

The initiate is oriented to sacred identity, devotion, moral character, lineage, and the first visible covenant with the Orisha current and Neteru sphere. These form the back bone of the Khemetian Tree of Life.

  • Amen / Olorun
  • Ausar / Obatala
  • Auset / Yemaya
  • Devotional purification
Summer Solstice

Gate II: Power & Warriors

The initiate learns discipline, protection, sacred boundaries, direction, action, and the difference between Ajagun training pressure and Ajogun affliction. Balance is enforced. Truth is flanked.

  • Herukhuti / Ogun
  • Sebek / Eshu
  • Geb / Ochosi
  • Warrior accountability
Fall Equinox

Gate III: Wisdom & Hand of Ifá

The initiate enters disciplined divination, memory, perception, sacred speech, Odu orientation, and right relationship with destiny. Integration of Oracles is wisdomic.

  • Tehuti / Orunmila
  • Maat / Iwa Pele
  • Sekert / Olokun
  • Divination discipline
Winter Solstice

Gate IV: Crowning, Priesthood & Implementation

The initiate integrates ancestors, ritual authority, service, royal responsibility, community accountability, and priestly embodiment. On the shoulders of enlightened ancestors, in the presence of Wise Elders.

  • Nekhbet / Iyami
  • Sheps / Egungun
  • Het Heru / Oshun
  • Global Priesthood practice

52-Week Structure

The 52-week expansion gives each the spiritual center time to become study, ritualize, practice, community serve, and demonstrate embodiment.

Weeks 1–13: Orientation, Devotion & Sacred Identity

Foundation in Global Priesthood, SGI purpose, sacred framework, Amen/Olorun, Ausar/Obatala, Auset/Yemaya, and devotional alignment. This phase prepares the initiate for the Spring Equinox gate, the Ilekes covenant and the Djed structure.

Weeks 14–26: Power, Protection & Warrior Discipline

Training through Herukhuti/Ogun, Sebek/Eshu, Geb/Ochosi, and the warrior function. This phase culminates in Summer Solstice readiness for Warriors, vows, sacrifice, boundaries, and accountability.

Weeks 27–39: Wisdom, Divination & Hand of Ifá with Tehuti Oracle

Study of Tehuti/Orunmila, Maat/Iwa Pele, Sekert/Olokun, sacred memory, Odu, divination ethics, and right perception. This phase culminates in Fall Equinox readiness for Hand of Ifá.

Weeks 40–52: Ancestors, Beauty, Crowning & Implementation

Integration of Het Heru/Oshun, Heru/Shango, Nekhbet/Iyami, Uatchet/Oya, Sheps/Egungun, and priestly service. This phase culminates in Winter Solstice priesthood crowning or crowning-readiness review.
